Training Overview

This TR-102 Summer Industrial Training provided comprehensive, hands-on experience in Python Full Stack Development and the highly specialized field of Agentic Artificial Intelligence. The curriculum focused on building autonomous and intelligent systems using the cutting-edge CrewAI framework.

Program Details

Duration: 8 Weeks Intensive Training
Location: Auribises Technologies, Ludhiana (Offline Mode)
